02 / Behind the projects

A little about me.

Hey, I'm BEANZ. I'm a self-taught technology enthusiast who's always enjoyed figuring out how things work — from computers and networking to old game consoles and the software that runs on them.

I learn by experimenting, testing, and building things I actually want to use. Some projects are practical tools. Others are a way to revisit the games and technology I grew up with.

I have a particular appreciation for simple programs that keep doing their job years later. If something can be straightforward, lightweight, and dependable, that's usually the direction I want to take it.

Find me on GitHub ↗

What matters to me

01

Useful over flashy

Make the core experience good before adding anything unnecessary.

02

Keep things understandable

Simple interfaces, clear purpose, and as few moving parts as possible.

03

Reliability matters

Test how the software actually behaves — especially on the hardware it's meant for.

04

Preserve the good stuff

Keep useful software and old-school experiences accessible.

03 / The BEANZ philosophy

Simple by design.
Reliable by implementation!

Good software doesn't need to be complicated. It needs a clear purpose, thoughtful decisions, and the reliability to back them up.
